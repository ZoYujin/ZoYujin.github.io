I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Taesup Kim in the Learning and Adaptation Algorithms Lab.

I study post-training of multimodal foundation models: how they acquire new capabilities without losing existing ones, and whether their reasoning stays grounded in visual evidence. Most recently, I completed a five-month research internship at LG AI Research’s EXAONE Lab, where I studied reinforcement-learning post-training for general-purpose vision-language models.

Before entering research, I worked as a journalist at The Chosun Ilbo. In both journalism and research, I have been happiest in conversations that help us understand one another and explore the world and intelligence, whether human or artificial.

● I am looking for research internships for Summer 2027.

Research

My work studies tensions in foundation-model training: preserving old knowledge while acquiring new capabilities, following language priors while remaining grounded in visual evidence, and allocating learning signal across competing capabilities.
